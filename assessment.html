Assessment & Test Design

Original assessments I created and used in my teaching — from grammar and reading tests to speaking and writing rubrics.

A sample of original assessments I created and used in my teaching — from grammar and reading tests to speaking and writing rubrics.

5 Pages

Reading Comprehension Test

  • Reading Comprehension
  • Inference & Detail
  • ACTFL

A five-page, ACTFL-based reading assessment that moves from everyday functional texts — job ads, orientation emails, and how-to guides — through opinion pieces and academic excerpts. It tests students' ability to scan for specific details, identify what a text does not state, and draw inferences about an author's stance in more abstract, higher-level passages.

Integrated Writing Rubric

  • Past Tense Accuracy
  • Essay Structure
  • Advanced Level

A one-page, 100-point scoring rubric for a timed integrated writing task at the advanced level. It evaluates how accurately and purposefully students use three to five different past tense forms, alongside source integration, essay structure, vocabulary, coherence, and mechanics — giving students a clear picture of what a strong timed essay looks like.

View the Integrated Writing Rubric

Past Tenses Grammar Test

  • Tense Practice
  • Irregular Verbs
  • Intermediate Level

A three-page, intermediate-level grammar test built around five exercises: an irregular verb form chart, a mixed past-tense fill-in-the-blank passage, two contrast exercises (past simple vs. past perfect, and past continuous vs. past perfect continuous), and five open-ended questions — moving students from recognizing one correct form to producing their own past-tense sentences.

View the Past Tenses Grammar Test
3 Pages

Listening Test – The Passive Voice

  • Passive Voice
  • Listening for Detail

An advanced listening and grammar assessment built around an original newspaper-style article on the life of J.R.R. Tolkien. Students listen to the article read aloud and fill in 26 gapped verbs with the correct passive form, choosing the right auxiliary to match the tense they hear — training recognition of the passive voice in connected, natural speech.

View the Listening Test – The Passive Voice
3 Pages

TOEFL Speaking Practice and Self-Assessment

  • Speaking Fluency
  • Self-Assessment
  • TOEFL

A three-page independent speaking packet modeled on the TOEFL iBT Speaking section, with ten advanced-level prompts and authentic 15-second prep / 45-second response timing. A simplified four-category rubric on the final page lets students score their own recordings for task response, organization, fluency, and language use — building the habit of self-monitoring progress.
