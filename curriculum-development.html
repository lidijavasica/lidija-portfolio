Curriculum & Program Development

Full-scale curriculum and program design projects, from literature courses to a new citizenship-teaching clinic.

A collection of full-scale curriculum and program design projects — from a semester-long literature curriculum for an Introduction to Serbian/Croatian Literature class to the administrative and pedagogical blueprints for a new Citizenship and English-teaching clinic I worked on.

Course Outline

Curriculum Guide: Serbian/Croatian Literature Class

  • 15-Week Plan
  • Literature Curriculum
  • Serbian/Croatian

This 15-week curriculum guide expands a university syllabus for Introduction to Serbian/Croatian Literature into a complete instructional resource. It opens with course purpose, learning outcomes, and a detailed breakdown of how reading assignments, writing assignments, presentations, attendance, and a final project are each weighted and assessed. A dedicated toolkit of classroom activities groups warm-ups, comprehension exercises, creative writing, and discussion strategies by function, giving instructors ready-made options for any unit. A sample 75-minute lesson plan format, illustrated with a full lesson on Nobel laureate Ivo Andrić, models how to structure class time around a Warm-Up, Present, Practice, Produce, and Wrap-Up sequence. The guide also includes a complete list of suggested literary works — novels, epic and lyrical folk poetry, folk tales, short stories, and modern poetry — each with an English translation. Its centerpiece is a week-by-week course calendar that maps specific readings and assignments onto weekly class sessions across the full semester.

Curriculum Guide & Instructor Training: Citizenship Clinic

  • Instructor Training
  • Course Design
  • Curriculum Guide
  • Team Project

Created as a collaborative team project for the Graduate Level Curriculum Development course, this document outlines the pedagogical foundation of the Citizenship Clinic, including its mission statement, program outcomes, and detailed goal statements for both immigrant participants and student instructors. It outlines a two-stage instructor training model: four weeks of pre-course training in teaching methods and legal boundaries, followed by ten weeks of mentored, during-course teaching with biweekly reflections and lesson plan submissions. A fully annotated sample training lesson walks through a two-hour session on citizenship and belonging, pairing each activity with the language-acquisition or teaching theory behind it. The ten-week course for citizenship-eligible immigrants is organized into four content units — Becoming a Citizen, The Government, Early History, and America Today — each interweaving Naturalization Test content, N-400 application practice, and spiritual content. A second fully annotated sample lesson, on the U.S. Constitution, demonstrates the model in action alongside a sample exit-ticket assessment. The document closes with a phased plan for what still needs to be developed to bring the course to full readiness.

Admin Plan

Administration Plan: Belonging Citizenship Clinic

  • Program Administration
  • Citizenship Clinic
  • Team Project

Written collaboratively with a team for the Graduate Level Program Administration course, this administration plan presents the full operational blueprint for a new citizenship and English-teaching clinic that pairs student interns with community members preparing for U.S. naturalization. It covers the intern schedule and course calendar, a complete syllabus for interns detailing learning outcomes, grading, and university policies, and sample lesson plans for citizenship-eligible immigrants built around the N-400 application and Constitution content. The plan also sets out an admissions process for prospective students, including an English proficiency screening flow, alongside seven legal guidelines that keep instructors from crossing into unauthorized legal representation. A full student handbook walks participants through the ten benefits of citizenship, their rights and responsibilities, and the ten-step path from eligibility to the Oath of Allegiance. Marketing strategy, target audiences, and a detailed cost analysis and expense breakdown round out the plan, giving the clinic a clear path from proposal to launch.
