The Feedback That Shapes My Teaching

Student voices from four semesters, across grammar, reading, writing, and listening & speaking.

Student feedback is one of the clearest mirrors a teacher has — it shows not just what was taught, but how it was felt, understood, and remembered. Across four semesters and every skill area — Grammar, Reading, Writing, and Listening & Speaking — these are the words of students describing what made their learning experience meaningful.
