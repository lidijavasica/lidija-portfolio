Welcome

Hello! Welcome to my Portfolio Website.

I'm Lidija Vasica, an ESL educator with over 15 years of experience and an M.A. in TESOL.

I also teach the Serbian/Croatian language, literature, and culture.

My focus is always the same: instruction that's practical, personalized, and genuinely effective.

Life in Pictures

A little bit about me

Ch. 01 Serbia · A Family of Seven

I am from Serbia, a small country in Europe. I am the oldest of 7 children! Growing up in a big family taught me a lot about team work, patience and the importance of communication.

Ch. 02 Europe · Classrooms & Languages

I have always loved reading and that lead me to study Linguistics and languages. I've been teaching English since graduating high-school. I got to travel around Europe while teaching in person and online.

Ch. 03 Utah, USA · Mountains & Master's

In 2023, I decided to move to the USA to get my master's degree in TESOL. So now I am living in Utah, surrounded by big mountains and good friends while trying to become a better teacher who helps students embrace learning as a life-long adventure!
