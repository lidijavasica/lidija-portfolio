Lesson Plan

Reading: History, Skimming & Scanning

  • Reading
  • Intermediate High
  • 101 min

Using a National Geographic video and article on Ancient Egypt, students built vocabulary while practicing the strategic reading skills of skimming and scanning. The lesson closes with a book discussion.

Learning outcomes: Students can use new vocabulary in context. Students understand the difference between skimming and scanning.

Lesson Plan

Vocabulary: Describing People

  • Vocabulary
  • Advanced Mid
  • 70 min

Students listen to Supertramp's “The Logical Song,” and complete a lyrics worksheet which helps them memorize useful adjectives. This lesson also introduced synonyms, antonyms, and noun suffixes through guided practice and pair-work interviews. Students then applied new vocabulary in writing.

Learning outcomes: Students memorize new vocabulary related to personality characteristics.

Lesson Plan

Writing: The Opinion Essay

  • Writing
  • Advanced High
  • 90 min

A structured session guiding students from linking words to a complete five-paragraph opinion essay. Students practiced drafting, exchanged peer feedback, and left with practical tools for organizing persuasive writing.

Learning outcomes: Students learn how to improve their opinion essays.
