Advanced Grammar Chapter

  • Grammar Curriculum
  • Online Textbook

A grammar chapter I created for an online textbook, written as part of a larger advanced-grammar project. It walks students through complex grammar structures with clear explanations, examples, and practice activities built for independent online study.

30 Prompts

Essay Writing: 30 Quotes, 30 Prompts

  • ACTFL-Aligned
  • Quote-Based Prompts
  • Writing

A collection of thirty original quotations from writers around the world, each paired with an ACTFL-aligned essay prompt across Intermediate, Advanced, and Superior levels. I created this list myself, drawing on my love of quotes, to give teachers a ready-made bank of discussion and essay topics for any proficiency level.

Course Curriculum

16-Week Course

16-Week Adult Beginner Course — Provo Community Class

  • Beginner Curriculum
  • Collaborative Project

A full 16-week intensive curriculum for complete-beginner adult learners, taught at a community class in Provo. I helped create this curriculum together with six fellow MA TESOL colleagues — the shared folder includes detailed lesson plans and extensive slide decks for every week of instruction, a rich resource for anyone teaching absolute beginners or planning a multi-week course.
