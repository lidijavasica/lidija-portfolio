Serbian & Croatian

Serbian & Croatian Language Classes

Syllabi, curriculum materials, lesson plans, and classroom resources for advanced Serbian/Croatian language and literature courses.

A collection of syllabi, curriculum materials, lesson plans, and classroom resources I developed and used while teaching advanced Serbian/Croatian language and literature university courses.

Syllabus: Serbian – Advanced Language & Culture

  • SRBIA330
  • Advanced Language
  • Culture & History

This syllabus outlines an advanced Serbian course that builds language skills through history and culture rather than grammar drills alone. Students read and discuss Serbian geography, history, government, religion and literature while developing communication, comprehension, and cultural knowledge. The course prepares students to take an advanced language proficiency challenge exam.

Syllabus: Serbian/Croatian – Introduction to Literature

  • SRBIA340
  • CROAT 340
  • Literature Syllabus

This syllabus introduces Serbian and Croatian literature from its earliest written texts through the modern era. It sets out course purpose, learning outcomes, grading, and a 15-week calendar covering folk ballads, lyrical poetry, fairy tales, short stories, and novels. Grading breakdowns and university policies are also included. The goal of the course is to help students learn about literature while simultaneously improving their language proficiency.

Curriculum Guide: Serbian/Croatian – Introduction to Literature

  • SRBIA340
  • CROAT 340
  • 15-Week Plan
  • Teaching Guide

This guide expands the 340 syllabus into a full 15-week teaching curriculum. It includes a detailed assessment summary, a classroom activities toolkit, a sample lesson plan format, and a complete list of suggested literary works. Its centerpiece is a week-by-week calendar mapping specific readings and assignments to each class session.

Sample Lesson Plan: Literature Class

  • SRBIA340
  • CROAT 340
  • Ivo Andrić
  • 75-Min Lesson

A complete 75-minute lesson plan built around Nobel laureate Ivo Andrić, following a Warm-Up, Present, Practice, Produce, Wrap-Up structure. Students learn Andrić's biography through video and audio clips, then work through a jigsaw activity based on his Nobel Prize acceptance speech. The lesson closes with a short written summary that reinforces past-tense grammar and new vocabulary.

Literature Class Activities & Discussion Questions

  • Classroom Activities List
  • Discussion Questions

A toolkit of classroom activities for Serbian/Croatian literature classes, covering warm-ups, comprehension practice, creative writing, debate, cultural presentations, and more. It's paired with bilingual Serbian/English discussion-question worksheets written for 20 specific works, including Na Drini ćuprija and the Marko Kraljević ballad cycle. Together they give instructors ready-to-use prompts for nearly any text in the course.

Teaching Resources Folder: Serbian/Croatian Literature

  • Suggested Literary Works
  • Poetry Audio Collection

A shared folder of supplementary materials for teaching Serbian/Croatian literature. It holds the full list of suggested literary works with English translations, a collection of folk works, a set of modern poetry selections, and audio recordings that pair with the readings. Instructors can pull directly from this folder when planning weekly lessons.
