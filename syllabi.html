Great lessons aren't found in the classroom — they're built before it begins.

Applied Grammar Class

  • Grammar
  • Advanced Mid
  • 14-Week Course

Moves students from spoken to written accuracy, teaching grammar principles they then apply to spontaneous speech and writing. Learning outcomes progress from noticing target grammar in context to using it confidently and explaining its reasoning across registers.

Academic Listening & Speaking Class

  • Listening & Speaking
  • Advanced High
  • 14-Week Course

Introduces students to basic academic speech, building skills to listen accurately and speak effectively in academic contexts. Learning outcomes range from understanding implicit meaning to producing organized, well-supported speech across major time frames.

Reading Foundations Class

  • Reading
  • Intermediate Mid
  • 14-Week Course

Builds foundational reading skills using simple, personal texts — from understanding main ideas and details to applying basic strategies like previewing and scanning. Students also grow vocabulary through context and practice reading aloud for fluency.

Reading Foundations Class

  • Reading
  • Intermediate High
  • 14-Week Course

Develops reading comprehension and fluency through simple narrative texts on familiar topics. Students learn to identify main ideas and details, preview and scan for information, and recognize basic organizational patterns.

Reading Class

  • Reading
  • Advanced Low
  • 14-Week Course

Strengthens comprehension of narrative texts through strategic reading — skimming, scanning, and inference. Students also learn to identify an author's purpose and point of view while building vocabulary and reading fluency.

Writing Class

  • Writing
  • Advanced Low
  • 14-Week Course

Prepares students for academic writing through drafting, timed writing, and peer review. Outcomes move from thesis statements and topic sentences to fully organized multi-paragraph compositions with clear transitions and conclusions.
