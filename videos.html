Classroom Video Library

Recorded at the English Language Center, Brigham Young University, Utah — a look at real teaching moments, from targeted skill instruction to full class sessions.

Video 1 · Excerpt

Teaching New Vocabulary

  • Vocabulary Building
  • Contextual Teaching

This is a 10-minute excerpt from a 75-minute Reading Foundations Class. After watching the video about history I introduce new vocabulary in context, which makes it easier for students to remember. I also help students expand their vocabulary by exploring word families and learning related synonyms, antonyms, and phrases.

Duration: 09:53
Video 2 · Excerpt

Book Discussion: Number the Stars

  • Inferencing
  • Summarizing

Another short 8-minute excerpt from a 75-minute Reading Foundations class. In this section of the class, we had a book discussion. Students discuss the characters and plot of Lois Lowry's Number the Stars, practicing inferring as they build new vocabulary. The discussion also gives students a chance to practice summarizing what they've read.

Duration: 08:05
Video 3 · Excerpt

Listening Comprehension: Ancient Egypt

  • Listening Comprehension
  • Speaking Practice

In this short excerpt from a Reading Foundations Class, students watch a video about Ancient Egypt, then we learn new vocabulary together and practice talking about the subject. This discussion also prepares students for a writing assignment on a related topic.

Duration: 09:55

Reading Foundations: Reading Strategies & Note-Taking

A full FC Reading class session: students watch a video on Ancient Egypt while taking notes on nouns, verbs, and adjectives, then move into textbook reading strategies for finding the main idea and details. The class closes with an extensive reading discussion of Number the Stars.
